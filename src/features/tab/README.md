# Tab editor (`src/features/tab/`)

Moved out of CLAUDE.md; keep it current when this folder's files change.

- `src/features/tab/` — the Tab page (`docs/specs/2026-10-24-tab-editor-design.md`;
  not in `sections.ts` until it is ready): `clip.ts` (the model: a clip of one or more tracks, `\track`,
  over the bars they share, `masterBars` holding each bar's signatures, repeats, tempo and section and each
  track's `bars` its notes; `Part` is one track with those marks in its bars, what the layout, drawing,
  edits and playback work on, made by `partOf` and put back by `withPart`, which keeps the other tracks'
  bars in step; the page shows, edits and plays the first track, with an "i" over a clip of more,
  `hiddenTracksNote` in `TabPage.tsx`; `docs/specs/2026-10-30-tab-tracks-design.md`; bars of
  columns, six notes each, durations in ticks, whole = 3548160, so a double-dotted
  thirty-second and a sixty-fourth in any tuplet of up to twelve notes (alphaTex's
  tu 3 … 12, or any ratio `tu (n d)`) are whole numbers too; grace notes take no time; a bar may change the time signature, `timeAt`;
  `parseClip`; `docs/specs/2026-10-26-tab-rhythm-design.md`),
  `ascii.ts` (`readAscii` with its errors, warnings and guessed durations,
  and `writeAscii`, the canonical form; `public/tab-ascii.txt` is the
  format, and its examples are tests; `src/__tests__/tab/fixtures/` holds
  golden input and output pairs for the spellings it forgives, see its
  README), `layout.ts` (pure: where systems,
  bars and columns go at a width, and `rowsFor`, the rows over the staff,
  each there only when that line uses it, each system with its own `rows` and `top`, `lineAt` finding the line under a y
  (`docs/specs/2026-10-28-tab-line-spacing-design.md`); a clip with `\systemsLayout` / `\defaultSystemsLayout` (`barsPerLine` in `clip.ts`), when View options' Clip's line breaks is on (`laidOut` in `viewOptions.ts`), breaks its lines there, squeezing before it wraps, and spreads each line by its notes (`spreadLines`; `docs/specs/2026-10-28-tab-line-breaks-design.md`);
  `docs/specs/2026-10-26-tab-note-marks-design.md`; with it, all pure on its layout: `layoutText.ts`,
  the words over the tab and the signatures, their boxes and presses on them; `hitTest.ts`, what
  any other press is on and where the cursor is drawn; `runs.ts`, marks over runs of columns and
  legato joins; `beams.ts`, which notes a beam joins) and `TabClip.tsx` (draws a layout as
  SVG, with the editor's cursor and selection; a column's numbers, joins and marks in
  `TabColumn.tsx`, the tab's own stems and rests in `TabStems.tsx`, its colours in `tabLook.ts`), `marks.ts` (pure: where each
  drawn technique mark is, so a press picks it, and its palette tool;
  `docs/specs/2026-10-27-tab-pick-marks-design.md`), `clipState.ts` (the clip
  with undo and redo), `editing.ts` (pure: the cursor, selection, frets and
  the edits that add, move or remove columns and bars, `normalize` keeping ties, hammer-ons and
  slides true, cut, copy and paste at the cursor; the palette's changes to notes are in
  `noteEdits.ts`, to words and bars in `barEdits.ts`), `tools.ts` (the symbol palette's table, shared
  with the keys) and `TabPalette.tsx`, `BarTimePanel.tsx` (it and `BarKeyPanel.tsx` keep inside the tab and close
  with `barPanel.ts`) with
  `TimeSignatureFields.tsx` (the time signature from a bar on, opened by a
  press on a drawn one or the palette; nothing in the header sets it),
  `WordField.tsx` (the field over the tab for a chord name, section or
  the capo, "Capo 3" over the first line; a tempo has `TempoPanel.tsx`: bpm in a `NumberField`, Tap, from
  `tapTempo.ts`, and a label, opened by the Tempo tool, a picked tempo's Edit or the score head's tempo, the
  first bar's, printed under the tuning, "♩ = 120" when unset; `TempoNote.tsx` draws its note
  everywhere; `docs/specs/2026-10-29-tab-tempo-button-design.md`;
  `docs/specs/2026-10-26-tab-words-design.md`, `2026-10-26-tab-rhythm-design.md`), `exportTab.tsx` and
  `TabExportDialog.tsx` (the Export options button; the Tab view's copy button copies the PNG;
  `docs/specs/2026-10-26-tab-export-design.md`, on the chord export's
  helpers), and `TabPage.tsx` (the editor, keeping the whole clip and editing its first track as a part through `clipAction` in `clipState.ts`, with its parts in `editorKeys.ts` (the keys on the
  tab, the staff cursor and the playback line), `useClipImport.ts` (import, examples, links and
  drops), `TabDialogs.tsx` (the Import dialog), `ArrangementsDialog.tsx` (Arrangements…: My arrangements, in folders one level deep, then the built-in ones in theirs, one line each by name; a click picks, Shift and Cmd/Ctrl pick several to delete or drag, a double click or double tap opens), `arrangements.ts` and `useArrangements.ts` (My arrangements in the browser, one key per clip, the open one saved as it is edited; imports, drops and `#tab=` links open as new ones; `docs/specs/2026-10-28-tab-arrangements-design.md`), `ClipActions.tsx` (View options,
  copy, Copy link and save; `shareLink.ts` writes the link, the clip's alphaTex compressed in `#tabz=` (its `\instrument` and FX line carry the Sound and FX), and reads it and `#tab=`; `docs/specs/2026-10-29-tab-share-link-design.md`), `WordTools.tsx` (a picked word's Edit and Delete, where fields and panels go),
  `jumps.ts` (pure: Ctrl/⌘+← → a bar, Home / End to the line's or clip's ends, moving the cursor or growing the selection; `docs/specs/2026-10-30-tab-musescore-selection-design.md`), `views.ts`, `notice.ts` and `useWidth.ts`: keys
  on the drawn tab, `docs/specs/2026-10-25-tab-editing-design.md`; import
  by dialog (typed, or Open file… for a local `.atex` / `.txt`), drop or a `#tab=` / `#tabz=` link replaces the clip, read on load or
  hashchange and then cleared, while a paste goes in at the cursor;
  Notation | ASCII Tab | AlphaTex, each with copy and save buttons at the top right of the clip, after the view switch (Notation copies the PNG; Staff | Tab | Both takes the top left) (`saveFile.ts`; Notation lists PNG, SVG, ASCII tab and alphaTex); Notation shows Staff, Tab or Both (`docs/specs/2026-10-27-tab-notation-design.md`): `notation.ts` (pure: pitch, spelling in the key, staff steps, accidentals), `StaffDrawing.tsx` (the treble staff an octave up, carrying the rhythm in Both, where the tab drops its stems), `staffEntry.ts` (pure: the staff cursor's pitch, the string and fret Frets picks for a note entered on the staff, Enter and Alt+↑ ↓; `docs/specs/2026-10-27-tab-staff-entry-design.md`; a press on the staff puts its own cursor there and hands it the keys, a press on the tab hands them back; Staff alone is edited the same way; with `moveNotes`, notes dragged by the head or moved with Alt+↑ ↓, Shift for semitones, ⌘ or Ctrl for the chord, `docs/specs/2026-10-28-tab-staff-drag-design.md`), `staffGeometry.ts` (pure: heads, stems and beams across a bar) and `staffMarks.ts` (pure: the technique marks on the staff and their boxes, which `marks.ts` picks, and `staffInk`, how far bends and octave lines reach, which `layoutClip` makes room for), `smufl.ts` (Bravura's glyph outlines, from `scripts/smufl-glyphs.mjs`), and `BarKeyPanel.tsx` (the key signature from a bar on, `\ks`, opened like the time signature's panel); the AlphaTex view is editable (the × in its text box's top right clears it), `AlphaTexEditor.tsx` (`docs/specs/2026-10-27-tab-alphatex-editing-design.md`: read half a second after typing, errors marked ⚠ and underlined, text as typed until an edit elsewhere changes the clip); Export options,
  the Export dialog, which also downloads the clip as alphaTex or ASCII). The clip's own format is alphaTex
  (`docs/specs/2026-10-25-tab-alphatex-design.md`; for agents
  `public/tab-alphatex.txt`): `alphatexRead.ts` reads it with alphaTab's
  parser (`@coderline/alphatab`, pinned to 1.8.4, loaded on first read;
  its renderer is not used) and keeps what Frets doesn't draw as written
  (`extra` and `header` in the clip), `alphatex.ts` writes it, and
  `readTab.ts` tells ASCII from alphaTex. `builtin_arrangements/` holds the
  built-in arrangements, one `.atex` or `.txt` file each in a folder per topic (its own README says how to add one), listed under Built-in
  in Arrangements… (or `/tab#example=<file name>`) via `builtins.ts`, `builtinFiles.ts` and the route `src/app/tab/builtin.json/`, shown in place of the
  clip and never saved (`clipState.ts` sets the clip aside;
  `docs/specs/2026-10-26-tab-examples-design.md`); each but the `samples/` must read with no
  warnings and write back exactly, and every newly drawn feature gets one.
  `playback.ts` plays a clip: alphaTab's MIDI generator and synthesizer
  (loaded with the chosen sound in the background when the page opens; `docs/specs/2026-10-28-tab-sound-preload-design.md`; the sound's samples decoded in a Web Worker, `soundDecoder.worker.ts`, asked by `soundDecoder.ts`, with `soundDecode.ts` the decoding both share; `docs/specs/2026-10-29-tab-sound-decoding-design.md`) with the SONiVOX soundfont in
  `public/soundfont/` (Acoustic Grand Piano is the Salamander Grand and Steel Guitar the Iowa
  acoustic and Clean Electric Guitar Karoryfer's, each its own soundfont loaded when used, `OWN_FONTS`; these three are the only sounds offered, built by `scripts/build-soundfonts.py`; `docs/specs/2026-10-27-tab-instruments-design.md`), through a small Web Audio output (`audioOutput.ts`; the metronome's and count-in's channels kept out of the effects by `dryChannels.ts`, which replaces alphaTab's mixing step so their voices render into samples of their own) and its effects (`effects.ts`: distortion, and fmlab's plate reverb `plateReverb.ts` baked into a convolver; set from the FX button after Sound, the two boxed together under the shown track's name (`trackLabel` in `PlaybackFooter.tsx`), `FxButton.tsx`: FX opens the FX dialog, and the light switch on its right turns every effect on or off; `docs/specs/2026-10-28-tab-playback-effects-design.md`); the Sound belongs to the clip too, `Clip.instrument`, its `\instrument` (`instrument.ts`: one Frets has no sound for is kept as written and played by the nearest of the three, a red ⚠ by the Sound list saying so), and the FX, each track's own (`Track.fx`, the shown track's in the FX dialog and in playback), written as a `// frets:fx` comment line at the top of its alphaTex or under its track's `\track` (`fxLine.ts`; `docs/specs/2026-10-30-tab-track-effects-design.md`), a run of FX changes one undo step, heard without stopping playback (`sameMusic` in `clipState.ts`; `docs/specs/2026-10-29-tab-sound-and-fx-in-clip-design.md`); `PlaybackFooter.tsx`
  is the footer under the tab (shown, it sticks to the window's bottom inside the card, `PinnedPlayback.tsx`; `docs/specs/2026-10-28-tab-pinned-playback-design.md`) (Play from the cursor, Loop setting loop points,
  Speed, a number field in `NumberField.tsx`, Transpose, the Volume knob in `VolumeKnob.tsx`, Sound) and the playhead (its line gliding with what is heard, `heardPosition`; `docs/specs/2026-10-26-tab-playback-design.md`); a right-click on Play opens `PlaybackOptions.tsx`, Stop leaves the cursor where it stopped, and a pause between loops (`docs/specs/2026-10-27-tab-playback-options-design.md`). `ScoreHead.tsx` is the score's head, drawn in the clip's card over the drawing (Notation only) as a printed score's first page has it (`docs/specs/2026-10-28-tab-score-head-design.md`): the title (the page's h1), the artist and "Words & Music by" centred, then the tuning ("Tune down 1/2 step") on the left, from `credits.ts` (pure, with the sizes in `HEAD` that the exported image's head shares); the tuning always shown ("Standard tuning" when it is) and a quiet "Add artist and credits" when there are none; a press on the title or the credits opens `CreditsPanel.tsx` (Title, Artist, Words, Music, the pressed one's field focused, written back by `setCredit`; issue #227) or `TuningPanel.tsx` (the common tunings and each string on the `KeyPicker`, from `tuning.ts`, frets kept) (`docs/specs/2026-10-29-tab-credits-tuning-design.md`); a guitar with all six strings tuned alike is written on the staff as fingered (`writtenStrings` in `notation.ts`). The eye button before copy, in every view, opens `ViewOptions.tsx`, each view's options under its name (`viewOptions.ts`: Notation's bar numbers on every bar, each line's first or none, and Size 50–200%, CSS zoom on the drawing and all over it, laid out at the width over the zoom; ASCII Tab's Rhythm line; AlphaTex's Line numbers; and, for every view, Colours: Theme, Paper, Sepia, Night or Custom's two swatches, drawn on a sheet whose tokens `paperVars` in `TabPage.tsx` sets), which the views, copies and exports follow; lines run to the full width but the last, and 4/4 beams plain eighths four together (`docs/specs/2026-10-28-tab-sweet-child-intro-design.md`). A double bar is `\db`; with `\multiBarRest` bars of rest in a row draw as one with their count (`multiRests` in `layout.ts`, each bar keeping its slice so the cursor steps through); under a staff the tab names a tied note only where a bar starts; the last line runs full when it has as many bars as the one before (`docs/specs/2026-10-28-tab-sweet-child-verse-design.md`). Under a staff a held bend's note, too, is named only where a bar starts, and a let-down note is named when a pull-off leaves it (`legatoStarts`; `docs/specs/2026-10-28-tab-sweet-child-solo-design.md`). A mordent (`umordent`, `lmordent`; `Note.mordent`, `mordentSign` in `marks.ts`, Bravura's sign) is drawn over the staff and in the vibrato's row over the tab, set from two Techniques buttons; a let-down note is named where a bar starts, and a tied note when a slide leaves it or under a mordent (`hiddenNumber` in `TabColumn.tsx`; `docs/specs/2026-10-28-tab-sweet-child-solo-two-design.md`). A tied note under vibrato is named within the bar too, a grace note bent up has no second number, and the staff brackets a tuplet no beam joins (`docs/specs/2026-10-28-tab-sweet-child-solo-three-design.md`). Every tuplet is bracketed, beamed or not; the wah's "+" (closed) and "o" (open), `Column.wah` (`wahc`, `waho`), are drawn in a row under the chord names (`WahMark` in `TabClip.tsx`) and set from the Techniques palette's Wah button; a let-down note that slides out keeps its number (`docs/specs/2026-10-28-tab-sweet-child-solo-four-design.md`). Two strings bent at once stack their labels, one per height, the top string's highest (`bendLabelLines` in `staffMarks.ts`), the wah's row goes under the chord names, and a tied note bent after a grace note bends from the grace note's number (`docs/specs/2026-10-28-tab-sweet-child-solo-five-design.md`). A held bend pushed on up, `b (4 6)` on a tied note (`Bend.from`), rises from its dashed line by an arrow with the new height's label, a step higher (`bendRaises` in `clip.ts`, `BendCarry` in `TabColumn.tsx`; `docs/specs/2026-10-28-tab-sweet-child-solo-six-design.md`). A tap is a circled T (`TapSign.tsx`) over its number on the tab and over the heads on the staff, in every view (`docs/specs/2026-10-28-tab-circled-tap-design.md`). A dead note may slide into the next note; a bend let down part of the way (`Bend.to`, `b (4 2)`) lands a step under its line, labelled, and a tied note pushes it back up from there (`bendLandings` in `clip.ts`); `Column.hairpin` (`cre`, `dec`) draws one wedge per run in its own row under the staff (`SystemRowMarks` in `TabClip.tsx`); an artificial harmonic (`ah`, `Note.harmonicFret` for `ah 12`) is labelled "AH" in the P.H. row and comes after pinch in the N key's cycle (`docs/specs/2026-10-28-tab-sweet-child-outro-design.md`). A free-time bar (`Bar.freeTime`, `\ft`) closes with a dashed line, and the first of a run says "Free time" in the tempo row with its time signature in brackets (`startsFreeTime` in `clip.ts`, `TimeBrackets` in `StaffDrawing.tsx`); a fermata (`Column.fermata`, `fermata medium`) is Bravura's sign over the staff, or over the tab alone in its own row (`FermataSign`; `docs/specs/2026-10-28-tab-sweet-child-ending-design.md`). A bend's arrows run its note's length, to just before the next note (`bendShape` and `PlacedColumn.bendRoom` in `layout.ts`). A picked note bent up is drawn on the staff at the pitch it reaches, after its starting pitch in brackets joined by a line (`Head.bendFrom`, `pickedBendSemitones` in `notation.ts`; let down in the same note, `Head.bendBack` after it, under a tab only); dead notes are a capital X on the tab; a ghost chord within a fifth shares one tall pair of brackets on the staff (`ghostChord` in `StaffDrawing.tsx`; `docs/specs/2026-10-29-tab-book-marks-design.md`). The whammy bar (`Column.whammy`, alphaTex `tb`) is drawn as printed guitar books draw it, from the number down under the tab to its depth in steps, under "w/ bar" (`whammy.ts`: where its lines and labels go, `whammyJoins` for tied notes it holds or returns; `Rows.dives` makes room under the tab), picked like a bend and set from the Techniques palette's Whammy bar panel; on the staff a tied note under it is drawn at the pitch reached and a pre-dipped one after its fret's pitch in brackets (`whammyGoesOn` in `clip.ts`, `barHeads`), and a dive no tied note goes on from draws a line through its pitches (`staffMarks.ts`) (`docs/specs/2026-10-29-tab-whammy-design.md`). `usePopover.ts` places and closes the small panels. `tabSettings.tsx` holds the page's preferences, a Settings section each (`useTabPrefs`: Playback, Metronome, Notation, Colours, ASCII Tab, AlphaTex, Editing, Export, each its own key, parsed in `PlaybackFooter.tsx`, `viewOptions.ts`, `editingPrefs.ts` and `exportTab.tsx`; Editing's "Fill short bars with rests", off by default, is the `fillBars` that `moveColumn` and `fillBar` in `editing.ts` follow), and builds Settings' Tab group on them, so the gear's dialog and the page's own controls change the same values (`docs/specs/2026-10-29-settings-design.md`). The note toggle after Loop (a music note with sound waves) sounds each note, alone, as it is entered (`docs/specs/2026-10-27-tab-hear-notes-design.md`). After it, Metronome clicks every beat (beat one higher; in 6/8, 9/8, 12/8 every eighth, each group of three marked), and its right-click menu (`ClickOptions.tsx`) sets a count-in of 1 to 4 bars before Play and the click volume: the clicks are part of the clip's MIDI (`countOf`, `withCountIn` in `playback.ts`), so they follow Speed, loops, repeats and time signatures, in a sound synthesized by `click.ts` for the Metronome page (#149) to share (`docs/specs/2026-10-28-tab-metronome-design.md`).
  Images and dragging are PR 3 in the spec.
