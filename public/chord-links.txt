FRETS CHORD LINKS
=================

How to open a chord, or a particular way to play it, on the Frets Chords
page (https://frets.eminor.net/chords). Written for people and for AI
agents. Everything below is a rule unless it says "may".

The Chords page shows one chord at a time: its name, its notes and its
shapes on a six-string guitar in standard tuning. A link opens it on a
chord, on a shape, or on both. For a progression, give one link per chord.


1. THE LINK
-----------

    https://frets.eminor.net/chords?chord=<chord>&shape=<shape>

Either part may be left out:

    ?chord=<chord>                 the chord on its first shape (an open
                                   shape when it has one)
    ?shape=<shape>                 those frets, named by Frets
    ?chord=<chord>&shape=<shape>   those frets under that name, even when
                                   the shape leaves out one of its notes

A chord or a shape Frets can't read is ignored; with neither, the page
opens on Am7.


2. THE CHORD
------------

A chord is its root followed by its type, lower case, with nothing between:
am7, fsharpm7b5, bb13, c (C major: the root alone).

Roots, one spelling per pitch, as chord charts write them:

    c  db  d  eb  e  f  fsharp  g  ab  a  bb  b

Write C# as db, D# as eb, Gb as fsharp, G# as ab and A# as bb. There is no
other root: no cb, no e#, no double sharps.

Types: the first column of the list below, exactly as written. "#" is
written "sharp" and a capital M (major) "maj"; the second column is how
Frets prints the name, the third the chord's notes above the root. A type
not in the list can't be linked: link its nearest type, or the shape alone.

3. THE SHAPE
------------

Six frets joined by "-", from the low E string (the thickest) to the high
e string: a number from 0 (open) to 15, or x for a string not played.

    x-0-2-2-1-0      Am, open
    3-2-0-0-0-3      G, open
    x-3-5-3-4-3      Cm7, barred at the 3rd fret
    8-x-9-9-8-x      Cmaj7, root on the low E string

Frets doesn't check that a shape can be played: keep it within about four
frets, one finger per fret, with a barre where a finger lies across several
strings.

5. CHECKLIST FOR AGENTS
-----------------------

1. Name each chord as a guitarist writes it, then find its type in the
   list in section 2; write the root from the roots list.
2. When the user asks for a particular voicing, add its shape; otherwise
   the chord alone is enough and Frets shows its shapes.
3. Check every shape: six parts, each x or 0 to 15, and its notes are the
   chord's notes.
4. Give each chord's name with its link.
