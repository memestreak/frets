FRETS ASCII TAB
===============

How to write guitar tab that Frets (https://frets.eminor.net/tab) can open
and edit. Written for people and for AI agents turning a picture of tab
into text. Everything below is a rule unless it says "may".


1. THE WHOLE TEXT
-----------------

    Title: <name of the clip>           optional, one line
    Time: <beats>/<note>                optional, 4/4 if left out
                                         (2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 6/8 or 12/8)
    <blank line>
    <one or more blocks>

A block is one line of tab across the page ("a system"). Blocks follow one
another in playing order and join into one clip. Put a blank line between
blocks. Any other line (chord names, lyrics, comments) is ignored.


2. A BLOCK
----------

    PM line        optional, palm mute (section 6)
    rhythm line    optional but strongly preferred (section 4)
    e|...          six string lines, highest string first
    B|...
    G|...
    D|...
    A|...
    E|...

- Exactly six string lines, in this order, nothing between them. Standard
  tuning only.
- Each string line starts with its name and "|". The names are only labels:
  the first line is always the high e, the last the low E.
- All six string lines are the same length. Fill every empty position
  with "-".
- "|" at the same position on all six lines is a bar line. End each line
  with "|".
- The rhythm and PM lines line up with the string lines character for
  character, counting from the start of the line (so they begin with two
  spaces, under "e|").


3. NOTES AND COLUMNS
--------------------

- A note is a fret number, 0 to 24, written on its string's line. "12" is
  one note on two characters.
- A note's position is the character position of its first digit. Notes
  played together (a chord) start at the same position on their strings.
  Everything that starts at one position is one column, played at one
  time.
- "x" in place of a fret is a muted (dead) note.
- Two notes on one string need at least one other character between them:
  "-" or a technique mark ("5-7", "5h7"). Digits side by side are always
  one fret ("128" is fret 128 and is refused).
- Prefer two characters for each sixteenth note of duration: an eighth
  four characters, a quarter eight. With a rhythm line this spacing is
  only for looks; without one it is how the duration is read (section 5).


4. THE RHYTHM LINE
------------------

The line just above the strings gives each column's duration: a letter
directly above the column's position.

    w  whole      h  half      q  quarter      e  eighth      s  sixteenth

- "." right after the letter makes it dotted ("q." a dotted quarter).
- "3" right after the letter makes it a triplet ("e3": three of them fill
  one quarter). Write triplets in complete groups of three.
- A letter above a position where every string has "-" is a rest of that
  length.
- With a rhythm line, every column needs a letter. Columns without one
  are guessed as in section 5.
- "|" in the rhythm line may be written above bar lines and is ignored.
  Anything else in the line must be a space.
- The durations in each bar should add up to the time signature. Frets
  draws bars as written; it does not move notes between bars.


5. NO RHYTHM LINE
-----------------

When a block has no rhythm line, durations are guessed from spacing: a
column lasts until the next column (or the bar line), and a bar's width
stands for one bar of the time signature; with no bar lines, the most
common gap is taken as an eighth. Guesses snap to the nearest plain or
dotted length. Leave the rhythm line out only when the rhythm is unknown;
the guess is a starting point to fix by hand.


6. TECHNIQUES
-------------

Written on the string line, around the note:

    5h7      hammer-on from 5 to 7     ("h" in the gap just before the 7)
    7p5      pull-off from 7 to 5
    5/7      slide up from 5 to 7
    7\5      slide down from 7 to 5
    /7       slide into 7 from below (no earlier note on that string)
    7b9      bend 7 up to the pitch of fret 9: 1 fret is a 1/2 bend,
             2 frets a full bend
    7b9r7    bend and release
    7~       vibrato ("~" right after the note, or after a bend)
    x        muted note
    <12>     natural harmonic at fret 12 (position = the "1")
    t12      tap at fret 12 (position = the "1")

"h", "p", "/" and "\" sit just before a note and join it to the previous
note on the same string, however many "-" lie between them ("5--h7" is
the same as "5h7"). The joined note's position is its first digit, so the
mark sits in the gap before it. "/" or "\" with no earlier note on that
string is a slide in from below or above.

In "7b9" and "7b9r7" the position is the "7"; the "b9" and "r7" belong to
that note and are not columns.

Palm mute is a line above the rhythm line:

    PM-------|

The run starts at "P" and ends at the last "-" or "|"; every column whose
position falls inside it is palm muted. A PM line may hold several runs,
separated by spaces.

8. OPENING IT IN FRETS
----------------------

Paste the text into Import on https://frets.eminor.net/tab, or make a
link that opens it directly:

    https://frets.eminor.net/tab#ascii=<the whole text, percent-encoded>

Percent-encode as JavaScript's encodeURIComponent does: a line break is
%0A, a space %20, "|" %7C, "/" %2F, "\" %5C; "~" is left as is. Keep the
link under about 8,000 characters; split longer tab into several links.
The part after "#" is never sent to a server.


9. FROM A PICTURE OF TAB: CHECKLIST FOR AGENTS
----------------------------------------------

1. Read the title and time signature if shown; write "Title:" and "Time:".
2. Copy each system as one block, string by string, top line = high e.
   Tab in pictures always puts the high e on top.
3. Line up notes that sound together at one position.
4. If the picture shows rhythm (stems and beams under the tab, or a staff
   above it), write a rhythm line: a letter for every column, rests
   included. Count each bar against the time signature.
5. Translate the marks: arcs with H or P to h/p, slanted lines to / or \,
   curved arrows labelled "full" or "1/2" to b, wavy lines to ~, "P.M."
   to a PM line, "x" to x, diamond or <12> notes to <..>, "T" to t.
   Leave out marks this format has no letter for (let ring, pick strokes,
   tremolo picking).
6. Check: six string lines per block, all the same length, every note
   followed by at least one "-", bar lines aligned.
7. Give the user the link from section 8 (and the text, in case the link
   is too long for the chat).
