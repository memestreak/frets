# Export size and transparency

Jeremy, 2026-10-17. Spec: `docs/specs/2026-10-17-export-size-design.md`.

1. `ChordDiagram.tsx`: `diagramSize` (width, height and frets of a
   diagram, used by the diagram itself) and `DIAGRAM_WIDTH`, five frets'
   width.
2. `exportStyle.ts`: `size` (one of `EXPORT_SIZES`) and `transparent` in
   `ExportStyle`, their defaults (300, true) and parsing; `BACKDROPS`.
3. `exportImage.ts`: `exportPixels` (the image's size for a shape and a
   style); `diagramSvg` sets that width and height and, when not
   transparent, a backdrop rectangle; `svgToPng` drops `PNG_SCALE` and
   draws the SVG at its own size.
4. `ExportDialog.tsx`: the Size and Transparent rows and the line under
   the preview.
5. `globals.css`: the line under the preview, and on a phone the shorter
   label and narrower options.
6. Tests in `export.test.tsx`: defaults and parsing, every size's pixels,
   the wider shape, the backdrop, `BACKDROPS` against `globals.css`, the
   dialog's rows, caption and saving.
7. The PNG line in the copy-chord-diagram spec, and CLAUDE.md.
