# Chord search

Jeremy, 2026-10-16. Spec: `docs/specs/2026-10-16-chord-search-design.md`.

1. `ChordTypeDef` gains `name` and `aliases` from tonal, so the search
   needs no tonal import of its own.
2. `src/features/chords/chordSearch.ts` (pure): `normalizeType` (one
   spelling per way of writing a type: ♭ ♯ Δ, spaces, brackets, ordinals,
   words in lower case, every letter but M in lower case) and `findChord`
   (root with its accidental, slash bass left off, type looked up in a map
   of every alias, symbol and name).
3. `SearchIcon` in `icons.tsx`.
4. `ChordPickers`: `searching`, `query` and `missed` state; the magnifier
   (`aria-pressed`, mousedown kept from stealing focus), the box (Enter,
   Esc, blur), the floating miss note and the `/` key; `onChord`.
5. `ChordsPage` passes `pickChord` as `onChord`.
6. `globals.css`: one fixed width for the dropdown and the box, the
   pressed magnifier, ink focus outlines, the miss note.
7. Tests: `chordSearch.test.ts` (symbols, case, every alias, written-out
   names, brackets, slash bass, root spelling, misses, no two types
   sharing a spelling) and a "finding a chord by name" block in
   `ChordsPage.test.tsx` (swap and find, one history entry, miss note,
   closing three ways, `/`).
8. CLAUDE.md: the new file and the spec.
