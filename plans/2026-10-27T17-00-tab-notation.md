# Plan: standard notation on the Tab page (issue #122)

Spec: `docs/specs/2026-10-27-tab-notation-design.md`.

1. Clip model: `Bar.key` (`{fifths, minor?}`), read from `\ks` in
   `alphatexRead.ts`, written in `alphatex.ts`; `keyAt`, `changesKey`.
2. `notation.ts`: open strings, sounding pitch, spelling in a key, staff steps,
   key signature signs, each bar's heads with their accidentals, accidental
   lanes. Tests in `notation.test.ts`.
3. `smufl.ts` from `scripts/smufl-glyphs.mjs`: Bravura outlines (via VexFlow
   4.2.5) as SVG paths in staff spaces. Credits in NOTICE.md and README.
4. `layout.ts`: `Parts`; rows for the staff over the tab; column widths that
   leave room for accidentals and seconds; the line's key signature room;
   presses on drawn key and time signatures; a press on a staff head lands on
   its tab note.
5. `StaffDrawing.tsx`: lines, clef, key and time signatures, bar lines and
   repeats, heads, ledger lines, accidentals, dots, ties, stems, flags, beams,
   rests, tuplet numbers.
6. `TabClip`, `PlaybackFooter`, export: follow the parts; bands span both.
7. `TabPage`: Notation view, Staff | Tab | Both switch (saved), Staff alone
   read-only, the ♯♭ tool and `BarKeyPanel` (the piano KeyPicker), `setBarKey`.
8. Example `key-signatures`; verify in Chromium (light, dark, 390px, export,
   playback); file the staff pitch-entry issue.
