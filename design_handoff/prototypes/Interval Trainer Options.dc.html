1Guitar interval trainer — three treatments

Same working quiz in each card (state is independent per card). Assumptions: standard tuning, low E on the bottom, root drawn in the steel accent, the interval note as a dark dot. Hold the primary button (or the H key) to overlay every interval from the root across the fret window; releasing hides it, no penalty. Targets stay within four frets of the root; interval names wrap at the octave. Wrong answers turn red and stay disabled until the right one is found. Settings and per-interval stats live in a collapsible drawer. Options vary the fretboard treatment and the answer input; everything else is shared. Open Interval Trainer on its own to use it full-width with the Tweaks panel.
