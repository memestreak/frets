## What the book prints that Frets didn't

- **Two strings bent at once stack their labels.** Bar 132 ends on a grace double stop whose two
  strings bend together, the 2nd a whole step and the 3rd a half; the book prints "full" over "½",
  their arrows meeting under them. Frets drew both labels in one place, on top of each other. Now a
  column that bends more than one string gives each height one label, the top string's highest, 11
  units apart; strings bent as far share one label. The tab keeps room for the second line when any
  column bends two strings, and the staff alone stacks its labels the same way.

- **The wah's signs go under the chord names.** Round 6 put the wah's row over the chord names, as
  the book's page 6 does; page 7 prints it under them. Jeremy, 2026-10-28: the wah goes under the chord
  names, and no articulation goes over them. The rows over the music now run capo, tempo, section,
  endings, chord names, text, then the wah, nearest the music; nothing over the chord names is a mark
  on the notes.

Everything else on the page Frets already draws: the wah's signs (round 6), grace notes bent up and
let down, held bends over tied notes and the bar line, a pre-bend, quarter-step bends, dead notes in
pairs, hammer-on and pull-off runs, vibrato and accents.

- **A pre-bend's label.** The book marks a pre-bend by its straight arrow alone ("full" in bar 132,
