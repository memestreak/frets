# Tab: a tap's circled T over the tab and the staff

Jeremy, 2026-10-28, after round 8 of the Sweet Child O' Mine round-trip
(`2026-10-28-tab-sweet-child-solo-six-design.md`): the book circles a tap's T and prints it over the
staff as well as over the tab (bar 141), and he wants Frets to do the same.

## What Frets draws

- **On the tab**, a tap's T sits in a small circle just over its number. The circle is filled with
  the paper, so the string line above doesn't run through it.
- **On the staff**, the same circled T stands over the column, in every view (Both and the staff
  alone; before, only the staff alone showed a T, and without a circle). It clears the heads, a stem
  that points up, and an accent or staccato dot over the heads.
- The staff keeps room over its top line for the sign, so a tap on a high note stays inside the
  drawing.

Tap entry and its alphaTex (`tt`) are unchanged.
