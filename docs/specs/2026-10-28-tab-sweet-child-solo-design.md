# Tab: a printed score's first solo (Sweet Child O' Mine, round 3)

Jeremy, 2026-10-28: the third round of drawing Guitar Pro's "Sweet Child O' Mine" near identically
(see `2026-10-28-tab-sweet-child-intro-design.md` for round 1 and the rules on copyrighted music, and
`2026-10-28-tab-sweet-child-verse-design.md` for round 2). This round is Guitar Solo 1, Verse 2 and
Chorus 2, bars 49–76, page 3 of the book. Verse 2 and Chorus 2 repeat what rounds 1 and 2 drew; the
solo's bends are what is new.

## What the book prints that Frets didn't

- **A held bend, under a staff, is named only where a bar starts.** A bend held over tied notes
  (`b (4 4)` on a tie) draws its dashed line at the bend's height, as before. In Both, the held note's
  number is left out within a bar, as any tied note's is (round 2), and drawn in brackets where a bar
  starts ("(9)"). The tab alone still names every held note. This changes what #144 drew for the
  Guitar Solo 3 excerpt, which named a held note within its bar; the two Guitar Pro sources differ,
  and the book is the one being matched.
- **A let-down note is named when a pull-off leaves from it.** A released bend's note (`b (4 0)` on a
  tie) still has no number, its arrow coming down to the string, unless a hammer-on, pull-off or
  legato slide leaves from it; then its number is drawn in brackets and the arrow comes down to it
  ("10 ↑ (10) ⌒ 8", bar 53).

## Not the same as the book, for later

- **Straight and curved bend arrows.** The book draws some bends' arrows straight up and others
  curved, from bend timing Guitar Pro keeps and alphaTex's evenly spaced bend points don't; Frets
  draws every bend curved.
- **A slur from one string to another.** Bar 48's last note (1st string) slurs into bar 49's first
  (2nd string). alphaTex's hammer-on joins notes on one string only, so it is left out.
- **Line breaks.** As in round 2 (issue #153).
