## What the book prints that Frets didn't

- **A held bend pushed on up.** Bar 143 ends on a full bend held over the bar line; in bar 144 the
  held note is pushed on up to a step and a half. The book draws the dashed line of the held bend, then
  an arrow rising from it labelled "1½", and a dashed line on at the new height. Frets couldn't keep
  the bend's lower start: `b (4 6)` was read as a pre-bend of 1½ and written back as `b (6 6)`.

  Now a tied note's pre-bend keeps the height it rises from (`Bend.from`, lower than its peak), and
  is written back as it was. On the tab the arrow rises from the held bend's dashed line right by the
  tied note, with the new label a step (11 units) over the first, and the dashed line runs on at the
  new height over the notes that hold it. The staff alone labels it like any bend.

Everything else on the page Frets already draws: the 8va line, the wah's signs under the chord names,
grace notes bent up and let down, held bends over tied notes, tuplets of 3 and 6, the palm-muted
slide out of bar 146, the Outro's section sign and dead-note chords.

## Not the same as the book, for later

- **A tap's T.** The book circles it and repeats it over the staff (bar 141); Frets draws a small T
  on the tab only.
- **Dead notes** are a capital X in the book (issue #177), and the issues of earlier rounds.
