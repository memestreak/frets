# The spec wins over the prototypes; Fretwood sets the look

Where a spec in `docs/specs/` and a prototype in
`design_handoff/prototypes/` disagree, the spec wins: the prototypes came
first, and the specs record the decisions made since. The prototypes are
not a reference for the look at all; the shipped styling is the Fretwood
design system (`docs/specs/2026-10-04-fretwood-design-system.md`).
Departures from the prototypes are listed in the feature's README.
